Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the AutomateEverything publishing tool ("the app") handles information. The app is a personal tool used by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered as a service to other users.

What the app does

The app uses the YouTube Data API through Google OAuth 2.0 to upload videos and set their title, description and visibility on the owner's own YouTube channel. Access is granted by the owner signing in with their own Google account.

Information the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses a Google OAuth access token to act on the owner's YouTube channel. The app does not read, collect or store personal information about any other people. It does not access contacts, email, files, or browsing data.

How information is stored

The OAuth access token is stored locally on the owner's own server and is used only to upload videos to the owner's channel. The token is not shared with any third party. No analytics or tracking is used.

Data sharing

The app does not sell, trade or share any data with third parties. The only external service it communicates with is Google/YouTube, in order to perform uploads.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time via the Google Account permissions page.
